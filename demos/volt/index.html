What they say

Before you book

Questions

I've never used a reformer. Is that a problem?

No. Book Reformer 45 and tell us at the desk. The coach sets your springs, walks you through the carriage before class, and keeps an eye on you throughout. Roughly a third of any given class is in their first month.

What should I bring?

Yourself and a water bottle. Grip socks are required and included free on your first visit; after that they're £12 or bring your own. Showers, towels and lockers are in the studio.

Can I train while pregnant?

Yes, with clearance from your midwife or GP. Priya runs the prenatal-adapted Reformer 45 slots and will adjust springs and positions each trimester. Please tell us before you book so we can allocate the right machine.

How late can I cancel?

Twelve hours before the class starts, free of charge, from the app or by message. Inside twelve hours the credit is used — with eight spaces per class we can rarely refill a late drop.

Is there parking?

Two bays behind the building on a first-come basis, plus free street parking after 18:30. London Fields station is a seven-minute walk and there's covered bike storage at the front.

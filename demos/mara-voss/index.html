FAQ

Good questions

I've never done Pilates. Can I start?

Absolutely — most of my clients started as complete beginners. Your first session includes a gentle assessment, and everything is scaled to your level from day one.

What should I bring or wear?

Just comfortable clothing you can move in and grip socks if you have them. Mats, props, and water are all provided at the studio.

I have back pain or an injury. Is Pilates safe for me?

In most cases, yes — Pilates is low-impact and widely recommended for back care. Tell me about your situation before we start and I'll adapt every exercise. If you're under medical care, check with your practitioner first.

How often should I train to see results?

Two sessions a week is the sweet spot. Most clients feel a difference in posture and tension within 3–4 weeks, and see visible change in 8–12.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your session — after that, the session is counted.

Do you offer pre- and postnatal Pilates?

Yes — it's one of my specialisations. Sessions are adapted safely for every trimester and for postnatal recovery, always in coordination with your care team.
